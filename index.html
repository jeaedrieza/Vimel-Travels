Travel Safety Tips

📱

Save Emergency Numbers

Download local emergency numbers offline before your trip. Keep a physical copy too.

📋

Share Your Itinerary

Always share your travel plans with family or friends back home.

💊

Pack a First Aid Kit

Bring basic meds, band-aids, sunscreen, and insect repellent.

💰

Secure Valuables

Use hotel safes. Don't flash expensive items in crowded areas.

🌊

Check Weather Alerts

Always check local weather alerts and advisories before traveling to any destination.

Get Travel Insurance
